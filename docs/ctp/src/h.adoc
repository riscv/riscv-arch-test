
=== H Hypervisor

The Hypervisor Extension adds an H coverage file containing new Hypervisor instructions, and additional coverage files related to exceptions, interrupts, endian, and virtual memory.

==== HSm and H Hypervisor CSRs and Instructions

HSm boots to M-mode and tests the hypervisor CSRs and instructions from M-mode.  It also holds the
mstatus.TVM tests of HS-mode and VS-mode, whose traps must be taken in M-mode because the HS-mode trap
handler reads satp, vsatp and hgatp.  H boots to HS-mode and tests hypervisor CSRs and instructions in
HS, VS, U and VU modes.

==== ExceptionsHSm and ExceptionsH Hypervisor Exceptions

ExceptionsHSm boots to M-mode with nothing delegated and checks that exceptions from VS and VU reach M-mode whatever
hedeleg holds, ecall and ebreak records, HLV/HSV faults taken in M-mode, and mtinst.  ExceptionsH boots to HS-mode,
walks hedeleg for exceptions from VS and VU, checks that exceptions from HS and U ignore hedeleg, and checks
hstatus.GVA, htval and htinst.  The misaligned cp_xtinst cases run only on harts that trap misaligned accesses.

==== ExceptionsHF and ExceptionsHV Hypervisor Floating-Point and Vector Exceptions

ExceptionsHF and ExceptionsHV boot to HS-mode.  In HS, VS, U and VU modes, with each combination of mstatus.FS/VS and
vsstatus.FS/VS, they access fcsr or vl and execute a floating-point or vector instruction that changes state, which raises an
illegal instruction when either field in effect is Off and otherwise sets both to Dirty.

==== InterruptsHSm, InterruptsH and InterruptsHGei Hypervisor Interrupts

InterruptsHSm boots to M-mode and checks mideleg, mie and mip with the hypervisor interrupts and mtinst on interrupts.
InterruptsH boots to HS-mode and raises VS-level interrupts through hvip, checking hideleg, hie, hip, vsie, vsip,
priorities and delivery to HS-mode and VS-mode.  InterruptsHGei covers guest external interrupts: hgeip, hgeie,
hip.SGEIP, hstatus.VGEIN, the SGEI priority and mip.SGEIP from M-mode.  hgeip is read-only, so only the platform
can raise a guest external interrupt, through the optional RVMODEL_SET_GUEST_EXT_INT and
RVMODEL_CLR_GUEST_EXT_INT hooks.  Without them, InterruptsHGei has no testcases.  The Sail configurations raise them
through the simple interrupt generator, and the Spike, QEMU and Whisper configurations through IMSIC guest
interrupt files.

==== ZicntrHSm and ZicntrH Hypervisor Counters

ZicntrHSm walks hcounteren from M-mode.  ZicntrH checks counter access from HS, VS, U and VU modes against
mcounteren, hcounteren and scounteren, and time with htimedelta in each mode.  ZicntrH exercises hcounteren for all
32 counters, so no separate ZihpmH suite is needed.

==== SvHSm and SvH Hypervisor Two-Stage Virtual Memory

SvH boots to HS-mode and runs guest code under two-stage translation: VS-stage and G-stage permissions, page sizes,
NAPOT and Svadu cases, guest-page faults and their htval and htinst, HLV/HLVX/HSV, and GPA widths.  SvHSm boots to
M-mode for mstatus.MPRV with MPV = 1 and guest-page faults taken in M-mode.  SvH also covers the SvnapotH and SvaduH
tabs.
