
=== H Hypervisor

The Hypervisor Extension adds an H coverage file containing new Hypervisor instructions, and additional coverage files related to exceptions, interrupts, endian, and virtual memory.

==== H Hypervisor CSRs and Instructions

H boots to HS-mode and tests the hypervisor CSRs and instructions in HS, VS, U and VU modes: CSR access and
walks, the VS CSR replicas, the hypervisor load/store, fence and trap instructions, and the virtual-instruction
exceptions.  The M-mode tests, and the mstatus.TVM tests whose traps are taken in M-mode, are the Sm suite's Sm_H group.

The H suite also contains the H_F, H_V, and H_VF tests.  They repeat the S_F, S_V, and S_VF tests from VS-mode and VU-mode, and cross
vsstatus.FS and vsstatus.VS with the HS-level sstatus.FS and sstatus.VS.  They run when F, Zve32x, or Zve32f is also supported.

==== H_Zicntr and Sm_Zicntr_H Hypervisor Counters

These are the Zicntr groups of the H and Sm suites, and run only when Zicntr and H are supported.
Sm_Zicntr_H reads every counter from M-mode while walking mcounteren, hcounteren and scounteren together, and reads
time with each htimedelta.  H_Zicntr checks counter access from HS, VS, U and VU modes against
mcounteren, hcounteren and scounteren, and time with htimedelta in each mode.  H_Zicntr exercises hcounteren for all
32 counters, so no separate ZihpmH suite is needed.
