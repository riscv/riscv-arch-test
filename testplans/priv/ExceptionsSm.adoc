[NOTE]
====
* `xepc`, `xcause`, `xtval`, `xtvec` and `mstatus.xPP` are inherently tested by every exception taken.
* Load/store misaligned, access fault and page fault for Zaamo, Zalrsc and Zicbo* are exercised in the PMA, PMP and VM suites.
* cp_misaligned_priority_fetch is missing from ExceptionsSm; the other Exceptions* test plans should also split cp_misaligned_priority by access type.
* `menvcfg.STCE = 0` makes access to `stimecmp` outside M-mode an illegal instruction.
====
