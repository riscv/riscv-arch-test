# Zvfhmin: rule-derived tests

`src/unpriv/zvfhmin.adoc` has one `norm:` rule, `Zvfhmin_dependent_Zve32f`. Generated by `tests_ai/gen/gen_Vzv.py`.

## How the extension is tested

- The dependency is checked through its observable consequence (vfadd.vv at SEW=32).
- The chapter's untagged defining sentence (vfwcvt.f.f.v and vfncvt.f.f.w become defined at SEW=16 with binary16
  operands) is exercised in the same file: vfwcvt.f.f.v of normal, subnormal, infinite, quiet-NaN-with-payload and
  signaling-NaN values (canonical NaN 0x7fc00000, NV for the signaling NaN); vfncvt.f.f.w with a tie, an overflow
  (65520 -> infinity), an underflow (2^-25 ties to 0) and a NaN payload (0x7e00).
- The test runs on harts that have Zvfhmin without Zvfh (e.g. `sail-RVA23S64`).

## Rules not tested here

None.

## Subtleties

- Whether other vector FP instructions are illegal at SEW=16 on a Zvfhmin-only hart is not tagged here and is not
  tested.
