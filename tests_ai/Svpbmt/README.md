# Svpbmt: rule-derived tests

Generated by `tests_ai/gen/gen_Sv.py`. The shared mechanics are in [../Sv/README.md](../Sv/README.md). RV64 only, Sv39.
This suite also holds `menvcfg_pbmte_op` and `menvcfg_pbmte_fence` (machine.adoc).

## How the extension is tested

`menvcfg.PBMTE` is set through T-SBI and followed by SFENCE.VMA x0, x0. The tests check:

- **Usable encodings.** PBMT = 0 (PMA), 1 (NC) and 2 (IO) translate in 4 KiB, megapage and gigapage leaves: aligned
  loads and stores, and fetches from PMA and NC pages.
- **Reserved encodings.** PBMT = 3 in a leaf, and any PBMT in a pointer, page-fault (S- and U-mode, every access
  type).
- **NC/IO aliasing.** Two non-cacheable aliases (NC and IO) of one page stay coherent.
- **Cacheable/NC aliasing.** A cacheable and an NC alias agree after `fence iorw,iorw; cbo.flush; fence iorw,iorw`
  (with Zicbom).
- **PBMTE=0.** The hart behaves as without Svpbmt: nonzero PBMT faults. PBMTE changes take effect after
  SFENCE.VMA x0, x0.

## Rules not tested here

- **Not observable.** The memory-ordering and coherence-loss rules (`Svpbmt_obeys_mem_ordering`, `*_obey_rvwmo`,
  `*_strong_io_ordering`, `*_treated_as_io_and_memory`, `*_may_weaken_ordering`, `*_fence_prevents_ordering_loss`,
  `Svpbmt_cacheable_aliasing_may_cause_coherence_loss`, `Svpbmt_aliasing_attribute`) concern ordering a single hart
  cannot observe, or are permissions.
- **Permission.** `Svpbmt_impl_may_override_pmas`.
- **Two-stage.** `Svpbmt_hgatp_stage_override_rule`, `Svpbmt_vsatp_stage_override_rule`: H suite.
- **No Svpbmt.** `menvcfg_pbmte_rdonly0` needs a hart without Svpbmt.

## Subtleties

- **IO fetches.** A fetch through an IO page may be refused, since an implementation may override more PMAs, so only
  PMA and NC pages are fetched from.
- **Cacheable aliases.** NC and IO pages are never accessed through a cacheable alias, except in the fence;
  cbo.flush; fence test. Mixed attributes may lose coherence.
